Goldberg privacy — version 0.8

Your repertoire, profile/avatar, practice history, notes, settings and badge dates are stored in the application’s private local database. No account, advertising analytics or automatic journal upload is provided. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled.

Smart Practice is optional. When explicitly started, it analyzes short-lived microphone samples on this device, including while the screen is locked or you use another app. The microphone hears ambient sounds, potentially including voices. Goldberg does not store raw audio, upload audio, transcribe speech, or identify pieces. Samples are overwritten after analysis. A microphone foreground service remains active while listening, including automatic pauses. Use Pause or Stop to release capture. Android permissions, privacy switches and power management can interrupt operation. Detection is experimental and may mistake other sounds for playing.

JSON backups and CSV exports contain personal information and notes, are unencrypted, and are written only to the document provider you select. That provider may synchronize files to the cloud. CSV is a report, not a restorable backup. Portable backups exclude unfinished practice drafts and device reminder activation. Restore replaces saved portable data only after confirmation.

Catalogue discovery sends queries to api.openopus.org. Biographies and portraits use Wikidata, English Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons/image servers. These services receive your request and ordinary connection metadata such as IP address. Goldberg does not send practice notes, microphone data or your journal to those services. Opening Spotify, YouTube, IMSLP or MuseScore links uses external services and their privacy policies.

Reminders are local, optional notifications. They can be delayed or blocked by Android. Microphone access never starts from a reminder, reboot or recovery. Uninstalling the app can remove local data; keep independent exports.
