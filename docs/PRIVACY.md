# Goldberg privacy — 0.10.1

Goldberg stores repertoire, profile/avatar, practice history, notes, settings, badge dates and unfinished drafts locally in its private app storage. It has no Goldberg journal server, advertising, analytics, session replay or crash-reporting integration. Android automatic backup is disabled. Data remains until you edit/delete it, confirm a replacement restore, clear app storage or uninstall. Keep independent backups.

Optional Google sign-in contacts Google through Android Credential Manager for account identity. Goldberg stores the selected account identifier, imported name and photo URL locally; an imported nickname/avatar can be retained independently of sign-in. ID tokens are not stored, logged or sent to a Goldberg backend. Signing out does not delete the local journal or imported profile. Google operates its own services under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); profile image requests go to Google image servers and obey the media download preference. You can use Goldberg without signing in.

Smart Practice is optional. After you explicitly start it, short-lived microphone samples are analyzed on this device, including while locked or using another app. Ambient audio may contain voices. Goldberg does not save raw audio, upload it, transcribe speech or identify compositions. A bounded memory buffer is reused and cleared when capture ends. Pause/Finish releases capture; automatic silence pauses continue listening. Android permissions, privacy switches and power management can interrupt capture. Detection is experimental.

JSON backups and CSV reports contain personal information and notes, are unencrypted and go to the document provider you choose. That provider may upload them. CSV is not a restorable backup. Published 0.10.1 format4 backups omit unfinished drafts, Google account credentials and device reminder activation. Recovery export is a separate explicit action. Restore replaces portable data only after confirmation; protect independent copies.

Catalogue queries contact api.openopus.org. Composer information and portraits use Wikidata, English Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons/image servers. Daily quotes may fetch Robert Schumann’s source text from Project Gutenberg; bundled quotations work offline. Requests disclose connection metadata (including IP address) and catalogue queries to those providers, but not practice notes, journal records or microphone samples. Network access can occur when opening these features. Metered portrait/profile downloads require the media preference; downloaded media is disposable and separate from journal data. Bundled Inter/Cormorant fonts make no web-font requests.

External Spotify, YouTube, IMSLP, MuseScore, GitHub and source/license links open services with their own policies. Reminders are local optional notifications; they never start capture. Goldberg sends no marketing email and sells no subscriptions.

Use Settings to manage permissions, sign out, export data and clear downloaded cache. Local edits remain under your control. Public project questions can be raised at https://github.com/stokonn/Goldberg/issues; do not post private journals, credentials or personal screenshots there. This notice describes the implementation, not a guarantee of compliance in every jurisdiction. Distribution-specific controller/contact, lawful-basis and children’s-service obligations need owner/legal review (see LEGAL-AUDIT-0.10.1.md).

## Unreleased Goldberg 0.11 Stage 3B branch

The Stage 3B development branch exports portable format5 with personal UUIDs, allowlisted settings, exact raw drafts/recovery archives and completion evidence. Imports save a full destination snapshot and retain original recovery bytes before journal replacement. These private recovery copies are not automatically garbage-collected; they remain until explicit later management, app storage deletion or uninstall. Failed preference application remains pending and can be retried. Valid drafts recover paused, with no automatic microphone activation. Unknown or conflicting drafts remain preserved for unchanged-byte export; completion evidence prevents restored drafts from receiving duplicate credit. Google credentials/account state, administrator privileges, platform permission grants and network credentials remain excluded. Previous cellular consent is inactive after import; desired reminders respect actual destination notification permissions. Backups are unencrypted and subject to a32MiB validation limit; oversized files are rejected without truncating personal data. The independent catalogue and downloaded media are not required for restore. This is not yet published production behavior.
