# User Guide

## Home

Published 0.9.1 shows configurable metrics and recent pieces. The developing 0.9.2 redesign shows today's practice, current streak, seven local calendar days, a daily sourced music quotation and recent pieces. It removes practice-start actions from Home. Zero activity is not drawn as a practice bar. Sessions use their recorded local practice day, consistently with historical statistics.

## Repertoire and catalogue

Search, sort and filter your pieces, add manually or explore catalogue information, and open piece details. Original titles, composer names and personal notes are retained. 0.9.2 pins search/Add and uses compact licensed composer portraits with placeholders when unavailable. This release does not expand or repair the entire catalogue database.

## Practice and review

Practice is the 0.9.2 entry point for starting sessions. Piece details select an intended piece and navigate there; they do not start timing. Timer calculations remain centralized. Multi-piece session parts and their notes are preserved. Pause/Resume and Finish & Review are the essential controls. Pieces/notes expand when needed. Unsaved changes are explicitly indicated; keep the app open and retry persistence when necessary.

Recovery never automatically restarts the microphone. If a draft cannot be read, retry or export the unchanged data. In 0.9.2, a separately confirmed fresh session first archives the original payload transactionally; failure to archive prevents the fresh session. Exported recovery payloads are diagnostic data, not ordinary importable backups. Full history loads incrementally and supports per-piece history without duplicate sessions for multiple parts.

## Profile, statistics and badges

Local nickname and avatar editing remain independent of any account. Goldberg 0.9.2 adds manually entered favorite artist, piece, era and biography, four compact statistics and up to five showcased earned badges. Tap the profile identity to edit it. The badge icon opens the collection; manage displayed badges there. Unlock dates and progress remain part of the journal. History has its own destination.

## Composer information

Catalogue data and original composer/work names are not automatically translated. Licensed small portraits may download on unmetered Wi-Fi; metered/cellular downloads need permission. Biography/large portrait content is optional and requested from composer details. Missing or unlicensed portraits use placeholders. Open portrait/source credits for artist, license and original source.
